# Your house, in your pocket.

Atrium is a native app for Home Assistant on iPhone and iPad, and the same house on the web. Open it and your home
is there: lights, shades, climate, locks, cameras, energy and music, laid out by room, the way you think about it.

**Your house as it is.** Rooms come from your Home Assistant areas and follow them: add a device and it is there.
Then Atrium's mapping skill has Claude make the calls a listing cannot — which camera is the doorbell, which
sensors are solar, which rooms matter most — in one small file.

**Works from anywhere.** Atrium joins your tailnet, so your phone reaches home wherever it is. No port forwarding,
no cloud account, no passwords: Tailscale knows who you are.

**Private by design.** Your house talks only to your own Home Assistant. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

**Feels at home on iPhone.** Built with SwiftUI, with Face ID before a door unlocks or the alarm changes.

## Getting started

1. Start Atrium and press **Open Web UI**.
2. Press **Sign in to Tailscale**. Once.
3. Open the Atrium app on your phone and scan the code.
4. Your rooms are already there. Have Claude fill in the Security, Energy and Music screens with the **atrium-mapping** skill (the set-up page says how).

Who can use it, and how the layout works, are in the **Documentation** tab.
